# Beispiel: outline

So antwortet der Skill in einem Chat ohne HTML-Anzeige. Mit HTML-Anzeige bekommst du dasselbe als gestaltete Seite: [`outline-beispiel-bericht.html`](outline-beispiel-bericht.html).

Alle Texte sind frei erfunden und dienen nur zur Veranschaulichung.

## Aufgabenstellung

> Outline the reasons the author gives for young people moving back to small towns.

## Ausgangstext

**Small Towns, Big Comeback** – by Laura Bennett, *The Northfield Herald*, 3 March 2026

(1) For decades, the story of rural America was one of departure. Young people left for college and never came back, and many main streets slowly emptied. But in the past five years, something has changed: towns like Millbrook, Ohio, are seeing their twenty- and thirty-somethings return.

(2) The most obvious reason is money. Rent in cities such as Chicago or Columbus has risen by more than 40 percent since 2020, while a family home in Millbrook still costs less than a two-bedroom apartment downtown. "We could finally afford a garden," says 29-year-old nurse Kayla Ortiz.

(3) Remote work has made the move possible for many. Software developer Ben Huang kept his job at a Seattle company after moving back to the farm where he grew up. Since the town received high-speed internet in 2023, he says, "there's no real difference between working here and working in an office tower."

(4) Yet the returners are not only following their wallets. Many describe a longing for community. In a city, they say, they hardly knew their neighbors; in Millbrook, people help each other and children can play outside without constant supervision.

(5) Of course, small-town life is not perfect. Public transport is almost non-existent, and there are fewer cultural events. Still, Mayor Diane Wells is optimistic: "These young people are opening cafés and starting businesses. They are not just coming home — they are rebuilding home."

## Schülertext

> In the article "Small Towns, Big Comeback" by Laura Bennett it is about young people. In the past young people left the small towns and the main streets emptied, but now they come back.
>
> First the author explained that rent in cities has risen by more than 40 percent since 2020, while a family home in Millbrook still costs less than a two-bedroom apartment. A nurse says "We could finally afford a garden". Then Ben Huang who is a software developer kept his job at a Seattle company. Because the town have fast internet he can work there.
>
> The author also tell that public transport is almost non-existent and there are less cultural events. But the mayor is very optimistic and I think she is right because young people make the towns better.

**1. Dein korrigierter Text**

> ~~In the~~ **The** ⟨voc⟩ article "Small Towns, Big Comeback" by Laura Bennett ~~it is about~~ **deals with** ⟨voc ①⟩ young people. In the past young people ⟨x⟩ left the small towns and the main streets emptied, but now they come back.
>
> First **,** ⟨p⟩ the author ~~explained~~ **explains** ⟨t ②⟩ that ~~rent in cities has risen by more than 40 percent since 2020, while a family home in Millbrook still costs less than a two-bedroom apartment~~ ⟨cop ③⟩. ~~A nurse says "We could finally afford a garden".~~ ⟨cop ④⟩ Then **,** ⟨p⟩ Ben Huang **,** ⟨p⟩ who is a software developer **,** ⟨p⟩ ~~kept~~ **has kept** ⟨t⟩ his job at a Seattle company. Because the town ~~have~~ **has** ⟨gr⟩ fast internet **,** ⟨p⟩ he can work there.
>
> The author also ~~tell~~ **points out** ⟨gr voc ⑤⟩ that public transport is almost non-existent and there are ~~less~~ **fewer** ⟨voc ⑥⟩ cultural events. ~~But~~ **However,** ⟨reg⟩ the mayor is very optimistic and I think she is right because young people _make the towns better_ (revitalize) ⟨~⟩.

*Erklärungen* (im Bericht zum Antippen am blauen i)
- ① „Es geht um“ ist ein Germanismus. Im Englischen ist der Text das Subjekt: *The article deals with / focuses on …*
- ② Im outline schreibst du im simple present.
- ③ Fast wörtlich übernommen. Eigene Formulierung, z. B.: *living in a small town is much cheaper than living in a city*.
- ④ Direkte Rede indirekt wiedergeben, z. B.: *a nurse explains that her family can finally afford a garden*.
- ⑤ Zwei Fehler: 3rd-person -s fehlt, und *tell* braucht ein Objekt. Hier passt *point out*.
- ⑥ Bei zählbaren Nomen im Plural steht *fewer*, bei nicht zählbaren *less* (*less money*).

**2. Inhalt und Aufbau**

*Inhalt*
- ✓ Wohnkosten (Absatz 2) und Arbeit von zu Hause dank schnellem Internet (Absatz 3) sind richtig erkannt.
- ! Fehlt: der dritte Grund aus Absatz 4, der Wunsch nach Gemeinschaft.
- ! Überflüssig: erster und letzter Absatz (Abwanderung früher, Nachteile, eigene Meinung) sowie Details wie 40 percent und die Namen.

*Aufbau*
- ✓ Jeder Grund steht in einem eigenen Satz und ist klar erkennbar.
- ! Du erzählst den Text mit *First … Then …* der Reihe nach nach. Gliedere besser nach den drei Gründen, verbunden mit *furthermore, in addition*.

*Darauf achtest du beim nächsten Mal*
1. Lies die Aufgabe genau und schreib nur über den gefragten Aspekt – hier die Gründe.
2. Gliedere nach den Gründen und lass Zahlen, Namen und deine Meinung weg.

**3. Sprache**

Deine Sätze sind gut verständlich. Es häufen sich Fehler bei Zeitform und 3rd-person -s, außerdem fehlen oft Kommas, und zwei Stellen sind abgeschrieben. Zu den Kommas hilft dir das [Merkblatt Kommasetzung](https://github.com/MrG-edu/Englisch-Bayern-Feedback/blob/main/merkblaetter/Commas_Merkblatt.pdf).

*Darauf achtest du beim nächsten Mal*
1. Schreib im simple present und denk an das 3rd-person -s (*the author points out, the town has*).
2. Formuliere in eigenen Worten statt abzuschreiben, direkte Rede gibst du indirekt wieder.
